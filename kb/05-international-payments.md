# International Payments and Exchange Control

## SWIFT Cut-off Times

Cut-off times differ by currency. A payment captured after the cut-off for its
currency is processed the next business day, even if the app accepted it. Quoting
a single cut-off for all currencies causes missed value dates.

| Currency | Cut-off, SAST | Value date |
|---|---|---|
| USD | 15:00 | Same day |
| EUR | 13:00 | Same day |
| GBP | 13:30 | Same day |
| AUD | 09:00 | Same day |
| JPY | 08:30 | Same day |
| AED | 11:00 | Same day |
| Other currencies | 11:00 | Next business day |

Cut-offs apply on South African business days only. A payment captured on a
Saturday in any currency is processed on the next business day.

## Exchange Control Allowances

South African residents over 18 have two annual allowances under SARB exchange
control. Both run on the calendar year and neither carries over.

| Allowance | Annual amount | Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Single Discretionary Allowance | R1 000 000 | Tax number, no clearance needed |
| Foreign Investment Allowance | R10 000 000 | SARS tax compliance status PIN |

The Single Discretionary Allowance covers travel, gifts, maintenance and online
purchases and needs no prior approval. The Foreign Investment Allowance requires
a valid SARS tax compliance status PIN issued for foreign investment purposes.

Minors under 18 have a R200 000 annual travel allowance and no Foreign Investment
Allowance.

## Fees on International Payments

| Item | Charge |
|---|---|
| SWIFT outward payment | R550 |
| SWIFT inward payment | R160 |
| Correspondent bank charges, OUR option | R350 plus recipient bank fees |
| Exchange rate margin | 1.75% Blue, 1.25% Plus, 0.65% Private |
| Payment recall or amendment | R650 |

A recall is a request, not a guarantee. Once funds have been credited to the
beneficiary, recovery depends entirely on the beneficiary bank and the
beneficiary's consent.

## What We Cannot Advise On

Agents must not advise a customer on the tax treatment of a foreign payment,
whether a transaction is tax deductible, or how to structure a transfer to
minimise tax. Refer the customer to a registered tax practitioner or SARS. This
is a hard boundary, not a matter of agent confidence.
