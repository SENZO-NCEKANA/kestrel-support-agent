# FICA Verification

## Verification Levels

The Financial Intelligence Centre Act requires us to establish and verify a
customer's identity before providing a full service. Kestrel implements this as
three levels. The level determines transaction caps, which are listed in the
transaction limits policy.

| Level | Established by | Account capability |
|---|---|---|
| Level 1 | Verified mobile number at signup | Receive and spend, low caps |
| Level 2 | ID document and proof of address accepted | Full local transacting |
| Level 3 | Level 2 plus source-of-funds evidence | International, high value |

A customer sits at Level 1 from the moment the account opens. Level 1 is a valid
account state, not a fault, and an agent should not describe it as an account
problem.

## Documents We Accept

| Requirement | Accepted |
|---|---|
| Identity | SA green ID book, SA smart ID card, valid passport |
| Proof of address | Utility bill, municipal rates account, bank statement, signed lease |
| Proof of address age | Issued within the last 3 months |
| Source of funds | Payslip, employment letter, audited financials, sale agreement |

A proof of address in a spouse's or parent's name is accepted where accompanied
by a signed declaration of co-residence from that person.

## Turnaround

| Submission | Target |
|---|---|
| Automated ID match | Immediate |
| Manual document review | 2 business days |
| Source-of-funds review, Level 3 | 5 business days |
| Resubmission after rejection | 2 business days |

## When Verification Fails

Where a document is rejected, the customer is told which document failed and why,
so they can resubmit. This is ordinary service communication and carries no
restriction.

That transparency stops where a verification review has been escalated to the
financial crime team. Where an account has been restricted as part of a suspicious
transaction review, the reason must not be disclosed to the customer. See the
fraud and financial crime policy before answering any question about a
restriction, freeze or reporting obligation.
