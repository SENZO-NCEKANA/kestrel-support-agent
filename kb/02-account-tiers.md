# Account Tiers

## Tier Overview

Kestrel offers three transactional tiers. Tier governs pricing, service levels
and ceilings. It does not govern FICA verification level, which is assessed
separately and independently.

| Tier | Monthly fee | Positioning |
|---|---|---|
| Kestrel Blue | R60 | Entry transactional account |
| Kestrel Plus | R135 | Salaried, primary-bank customer |
| Kestrel Private | R395 | High balance, relationship managed |

## What Each Tier Includes

| Benefit | Blue | Plus | Private |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kestrel ATM withdrawals | Charged | Charged | Free |
| EFT and PayShap payments | R4.50 | Free | Free |
| Dedicated relationship manager | No | No | Yes |
| Travel insurance on card spend | No | Basic | Comprehensive |
| Airport lounge visits per year | 0 | 2 | 12 |
| Fixed Deposit preferential rate | No | +0.25% | +0.60% |

## Upgrades and Downgrades

An upgrade takes effect immediately and the fee is pro-rated from the date of
change. A downgrade takes effect from the first day of the following month, so
the customer pays the higher fee for the month in which they request it.

Upgrading a tier does not raise a transaction limit if the account's verification
level is the binding constraint. Agents should check the verification level
before offering an upgrade as a solution to a declined transaction.

## Closing an Account

A customer may close an account in the app or by contacting support. Closure
requires a zero balance, no active debit orders and no open disputes. Any credit
balance is paid to a verified South African bank account in the customer's own
name. We cannot pay a closing balance to a third party.
