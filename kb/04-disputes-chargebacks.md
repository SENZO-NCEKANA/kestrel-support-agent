# Disputes and Chargebacks

## Dispute Windows by Reason

The window to lodge a dispute depends on the reason for the dispute, not on the
transaction date alone. Quoting a single window for all disputes is the most
frequent error on this topic and can cost a customer a valid claim.

| Dispute reason | Window from transaction date |
|---|---|
| Unauthorised or fraudulent transaction | 30 days |
| Duplicate or incorrect amount charged | 60 days |
| Goods or services not received | 120 days |
| Goods received not as described | 120 days |
| Cancelled subscription still charging | 90 days |
| ATM cash not dispensed | 30 days |
| Credit not processed after a refund promise | 90 days |

The 120-day window for goods not received runs from the transaction date, not
from the promised delivery date. Where a merchant gave a delivery date beyond
120 days from payment, the window runs from that expected delivery date instead.

## What We Need to Lodge a Dispute

A dispute cannot be lodged without the transaction reference and the reason code.
For merchandise disputes we also need proof the customer attempted to resolve the
matter with the merchant first, unless the merchant is unreachable or insolvent.

| Requirement | Fraud | Merchandise | Subscription |
|---|---|---|---|
| Transaction reference | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Merchant contact attempted | No | Yes | Yes |
| Cancellation confirmation | No | No | Yes |
| Police case number | Over R20 000 | No | No |

## Provisional Credit

Where a fraud dispute is lodged within the 30-day window, a provisional credit is
raised within 2 business days while the investigation runs. Provisional credit is
not a final outcome and is reversed if the dispute is not upheld.

Merchandise disputes do not attract provisional credit. The funds move only when
the chargeback succeeds or the merchant concedes.

## Timelines

| Stage | Target |
|---|---|
| Acknowledgement | 1 business day |
| Provisional credit, fraud only | 2 business days |
| Merchant response period | 30 days |
| Final outcome | 45 days from lodging |

If a dispute is declined, the customer may ask for the decline reason in writing
and may escalate to the National Financial Ombud. Never discourage a customer from
escalating and never present an internal decline as final and unappealable.
