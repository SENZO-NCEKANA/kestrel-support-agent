# Fee Schedule

## Monthly Account Fees

| Account | Monthly fee | Waiver threshold |
|---|---|---|
| Kestrel Blue | R60 | R25 000 average balance |
| Kestrel Plus | R135 | R50 000 average balance |
| Kestrel Private | R395 | R500 000 average balance |

The waiver is assessed on the average balance across the calendar month, not the
closing balance. A customer who moves money in on the last day of the month does
not qualify for that month's waiver.

## What Counts Toward the Waiver Threshold

Balances in a Kestrel Fixed Deposit do count toward the monthly fee waiver
threshold. Balances in a Kestrel Vault savings pocket do not. The distinction
catches customers out constantly and is our most common pricing complaint.

| Product | Counts toward waiver |
|---|---|
| Cheque or transactional balance | Yes |
| Fixed Deposit | Yes |
| Notice Deposit | Yes |
| Vault savings pocket | No |
| Credit facility balance | No |

A customer holding R30 000 in a Vault pocket on a Blue account is still charged
the R60 monthly fee, because no part of the Vault balance counts.

## Transaction Fees

| Transaction | Blue | Plus | Private |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kestrel ATM withdrawal | R12 per R1 000 | R10 per R1 000 | Free |
| Other bank ATM withdrawal | R25 per R1 000 | R22 per R1 000 | R10 per R1 000 |
| EFT or PayShap payment | R4.50 | Free | Free |
| Unpaid debit order | R85 | R85 | R85 |
| Immediate interbank payment | R45 | R35 | Free |

The unpaid debit order fee of R85 is charged when a debit order presented against
the account is returned for insufficient funds. It is charged per presentation,
so an order re-presented twice in one month attracts the fee twice.

## Card Fees

| Card service | Blue | Plus | Private |
|---|---|---|---|
| Replacement card, lost or damaged | R150 | R150 | Free |
| Replacement card, expired | Free | Free | Free |
| Additional linked card | R100 | R100 | Free |
| Emergency card delivery | R350 | R350 | R150 |

Replacement is free on all tiers where the card is reissued because of confirmed
fraud on the account. The table above does not apply in that case and must never
be quoted to a customer who has reported fraud on their card.

## Fee Reversals

An agent may reverse one unpaid debit order fee per customer per twelve months as
a goodwill gesture, recorded against the ticket. Anything beyond that needs team
leader approval. Monthly account fees are never reversed at agent level.
