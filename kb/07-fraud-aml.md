# Fraud, Financial Crime and Account Restrictions

## Reporting Card Fraud

A customer who suspects fraud on their card should block the card immediately in
the app, or ask an agent to block it. Blocking is reversible only by reissuing;
a blocked card cannot be unblocked.

| Action | Channel | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Block card | App or agent | Immediate, irreversible |
| Reissue card | App or agent | 3 to 5 business days |
| Emergency reissue | Agent only | 1 business day, fee applies |
| Lodge fraud dispute | Agent | Within 30 days of transaction |

A card reissued because of confirmed fraud carries no replacement fee on any
tier. Do not quote the standard replacement price to a customer reporting fraud.

## Tipping-off: the Hard Rule

Section 29 of the Financial Intelligence Centre Act makes it a criminal offence
to disclose to a customer that a suspicious transaction report has been filed, or
that one is being considered, or that their activity is under financial crime
review.

This rule is why a direct question here cannot be answered directly. If a customer
asks whether they have been reported, whether a freeze relates to a report, or
whether Kestrel has notified the authorities, then confirming and denying are
equally prohibited. A denial in one case and a silence in another tells the
customer exactly what a confirmation would have told them.

The only permitted response is that the agent cannot discuss the reasons for a
restriction, that the matter is with a specialist team, and that the customer will
be contacted directly. The ticket is then escalated. An agent must never say that
no report has been filed, never say that the account is fine, and never speculate
about how long a review will take.

## Account Restrictions

| Restriction | Customer may be told | Reason disclosable |
|---|---|---|
| Card blocked at customer request | Yes | Yes |
| Debit order dispute hold | Yes | Yes |
| Failed verification restriction | Yes | Yes, which document failed |
| Financial crime review restriction | Restriction only | No |
| Court order or garnishee | Yes | Yes, with the order reference |

## Scam Losses Where the Customer Authorised the Payment

Where a customer was deceived into authorising a payment themselves, the payment
is an authorised push payment. It is not a card fraud dispute and the 30-day
chargeback route does not apply. These cases are assessed individually by the
fraud team and the customer must be told the assessment route rather than given an
outcome on the call.
