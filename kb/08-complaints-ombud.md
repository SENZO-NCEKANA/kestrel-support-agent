# Complaints and Ombud Referral

## Internal Complaints Process

Every complaint is logged with a reference on first contact, whatever the channel.
A customer is entitled to that reference immediately and to a written outcome at
the end.

| Stage | Owner | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Acknowledgement with reference | Agent | Same business day |
| First substantive response | Case handler | 5 business days |
| Complex or multi-product case | Case handler | 20 business days |
| Final response letter | Complaints manager | 40 business days |

## Referral to the National Financial Ombud

A customer may refer a complaint to the National Financial Ombud Scheme South
Africa once they have a final response, or once 40 business days have passed with
no final response, whichever comes first.

| Right | Detail |
|---|---|
| Time to refer after final response | 6 months |
| Cost to the customer | Free |
| Binding on Kestrel | Yes, where the determination is accepted by the customer |
| Binding on the customer | No, the customer may still litigate |

Agents must inform a customer of this right whenever a complaint reaches a final
response or the 40-day mark. Discouraging referral, presenting it as a last
resort, or implying it will delay resolution is prohibited.

## What Is Not a Complaint

A request for information, a limit increase, or a fee reversal is a service
request, not a complaint. It becomes a complaint when the customer expresses
dissatisfaction with an outcome or with how they were treated. When in doubt, log
it as a complaint — under-logging is the failure mode that damages customers.

## Advice We Cannot Give

Kestrel support agents are not authorised financial services providers under the
Financial Advisory and Intermediary Services Act. Agents must not recommend a
product as suitable for a customer's circumstances, advise on whether to invest,
switch or withdraw, or comment on the merits of a competitor's product.

Explaining how a product works factually is permitted. Recommending that a
particular customer take it is advice, and requires an accredited representative.
Refer these to a Kestrel financial adviser.
